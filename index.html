// bug bounty experience

  
Smart-contract and protocol findings submitted to public bug bounty programs on Cantina, Immunefi and HackenProof — each with a runnable proof of concept. Across EVM, Cosmos SDK (Go) and TON (FunC).

  

    
sevprogramfindingstackplatform

    

      critical
      OKX Labs DEX router
      Unrestricted public swap entry point lets anyone route the router's residual ERC20 balance to themselves
      solidity
      cantina · dup
    

    

      critical
      OKX Labs DEX router
      Commission library pays the referrer the router's full token balance instead of only the pulled commission
      solidity · yul
      cantina · dup
    

    

      high
      dYdX v4 chain
      Float64 precision loss in market listing makes atomicResolution off by one — permanent 10× wrong margins in permissionless markets
      go · cosmos
      cantina · dup
    

    

      high
      SSV Network
      Operator removal with active validators orphans them — permanent freezing of staked ETH
      solidity · staking
      immunefi · dup
    

    

      high
      Whitechain bridge
      Relayer ECDSA hash omits the mapping id — a signature for one token mapping is replayable on another
      solidity · bridge
      hackenproof · dup
    

    

      —
      STON.fi DEX v2
      Pool and router silently drop bounced messages — reserves desync and funds are orphaned on failed transfers
      func · ton
      hackenproof · dup

Stock-counting and accounting app for an auto parts depot. Staff scan QR labels on a handheld Android terminal, count shelves offline and sync when back online; the office runs the same data on a desktop app. Supabase is the single source of truth, with per-employee roles.
